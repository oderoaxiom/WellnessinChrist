Core Principle

The Danger of Private Interpretation

2 Peter 1:20

Scripture teaches that no prophecy of Scripture is of any private interpretation. When we isolate verses without balancing them against the full counsel of God, we veer into error.

By adhering strictly to precept upon precept, the Holy Spirit unveils the seamless tapestry of truth, protecting believers from deception.

Spiritual Warfare

"It Is Written, And It Is Written Again"

Matthew 4:4, 7

When our Lord Jesus was tempted in the wilderness, He did not rely on single-sided thoughts or emotional reasoning. He answered Satan with absolute fidelity to the written Word.

Furthermore, when Satan quoted Scripture, Jesus countered with "It is written again"—demonstrating that complete biblical truth requires comprehensive harmony.

Holistic Health

Wellness in Christ

3 John 1:2

True wellness encompasses spirit, soul, and body. At Wellness in Christ Fellowship, we live and do as it is written, anchoring our health and spiritual vitality in obedience to God's commandments.
